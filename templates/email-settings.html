📋 SMTP Setup Instructions

For Gmail (Recommended)

  1. Go to your Google Account: myaccount.google.com
  2. Navigate to Security → 2-Step Verification (must be enabled)
  3. At the bottom, click App passwords
  4. Select app: Mail, Select device: Other → name it "Badminton Tournament"
  5. Click Generate — copy the 16-character password
  6. Use these settings:
    • SMTP Host: smtp.gmail.com
    • SMTP Port: 587
    • Email: your Gmail address
    • Password: the 16-character app password (NOT your Gmail password)

For Outlook/Hotmail

  • SMTP Host: smtp.office365.com
  • SMTP Port: 587
  • Email: your Outlook address
  • Password: your account password (or app password if 2FA enabled)

For Custom Domain

  • Check with your email provider for SMTP host and port
  • Common ports: 587 (TLS) or 465 (SSL)
⚠️ Important: Never use your regular password. Always use an App Password for security.
